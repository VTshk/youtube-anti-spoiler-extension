## 1. Play icon centred behind the band, as in the logo

- Remove the triangle from the left of the name. "Anti-Spoiler" stays in the cap, left-aligned, 19 px / 800, white.
- Draw the white triangle horizontally centred in the card. It starts inside the cap and continues down **behind** the
  dark band, so the band covers roughly its lower half, just like `icons/icon.svg`. In the logo the triangle spans
  y 21–48 and the band starts at y 35.
- Size: about 28–32 px tall. Choose a size that reads well at 352 px width and does not touch the name.
- Use plain positioning: an absolutely positioned inline SVG or a CSS triangle under the band in z-order. The band text
  and switch stay readable on top.
- The triangle is decoration: `aria-hidden="true"`.

## 2. Clicking anywhere on the card toggles protection

- The whole hero card (cap, band and bottom strip) acts as the master switch.
- The simplest correct way: make the card element itself the `<label>` of the existing master
  `<input type="checkbox" role="switch">`. A click anywhere then toggles it natively, and the existing `change`
  handler saves.
- Do NOT add a second click handler that toggles too, or one click would toggle twice. A click on the switch itself
  must still toggle exactly once.
- Cursor: `pointer` on the whole card.
- Keyboard: unchanged. The switch input stays focusable, and Space toggles it. The focus ring stays visible as now.
- Accessibility: the switch keeps its `aria-label`. Make sure wrapping it in a label does not change its accessible
  name in a bad way; if it does, keep `aria-label` on the input, which takes precedence.
- Read-only mode (settings failed to load): the input is disabled, so clicks on the card do nothing. Keep it that way,
  and do not show the pointer cursor then.

## 3. Hover effect

- **Protection off (gray card):** while the pointer is over the card, a soft circle of the brand red (`#c85252`) about
  70–90 px in radius follows the cursor across the gray cap and strip, fading out to gray at its edge.
  - It is a hint that clicking turns protection back on.
  - Implement it with a pseudo-element or overlay coloured `#c85252` and a
    `mask-image: radial-gradient(circle <r> at var(--x) var(--y), #000, transparent)`, which shows only near the
    pointer.
  - `opacity: 0` normally, then `1` on `:hover` with a ~150 ms transition.
  - One `pointermove` listener on the card sets `--x` / `--y` in px relative to the card. Nothing else runs per move:
    no layout reads besides one `getBoundingClientRect` per move, which is fine at this size.
  - The dark band and its text stay on top and readable. The effect colours the cap and bottom strip areas; it may
    also tint behind the band as long as the text stays readable. Choose what looks right, and say what you chose.
- **Protection on:** a subtle hover only. For example, the band gets slightly lighter (`#2a2a2a`), or the switch knob
  gets a slight glow. Keep it calm.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`: no transition. The reveal may still follow the cursor without fading.
- No hover effect in read-only mode.
