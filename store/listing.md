# Chrome Web Store Listing - Anti-Spoiler for YouTube™

## Name

Anti-Spoiler for YouTube™ – Hide Video Length & Progress Bar

## Short Description

Hide video length and the progress bar on YouTube to avoid spoilers in sports and esports replays.

## Detailed Description

Want to hide the video length on YouTube because it gives away whether a match ends early or goes the distance? The progress bar can spoil a match before it ends. Watch sports and esports replays, VODs and streams with those clues covered.

Anti-Spoiler for YouTube™ keeps result-revealing details out of view while you watch.

What stays covered
• Video time and duration in the player, thumbnails, search results and recommendations, plus chapter and transcript timestamps.
• The progress bar, replaced by skip buttons: back or forward 10 seconds, 1 minute or 10 minutes.
• Comments behind a "Show comments" button.
• Optional blur for thumbnails, previews and end screens.
• Optional title blur, including the open video's title and browser tab.

Ready from the start
Protection, time hiding, "Hide progress bar", "Hide comments" and "Protect live streams" (Beta) are on by default. "Blur previews" and "Blur video titles" are off by default. Adjust these settings in the popup.

Your controls
Open the extension popup to adjust settings. Alt+Shift+S turns protection on or off.
Already know the result? Click the eye button in the player or press Alt+Shift+V. This reveals the open video's time, timeline and comments; other videos stay protected. Use the same control to cover it again.

Live streams (Beta)
The player is covered and muted until you choose "Start from beginning" or "Continue live". The first option uses the earliest point available in the stream's rewind window. If rewinding is disabled, only continuing live is possible. Live chat stays behind "Show comments".

Privacy and languages
Protection runs locally, with no analytics or requests to external services. It uses only the storage permission and access to https://www.youtube.com/*. Settings can sync through Chrome when browser sync is enabled; language and theme choices stay on your device. The page's local storage keeps your two blur choices so the blur applies at once, and the tab's session storage remembers which video you uncovered until you leave it or close the tab.

Choose from 10 interface languages: English, Russian, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), German, French, Japanese, Korean, Indonesian and Turkish.
